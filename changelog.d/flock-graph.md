### Added
- Selecting an agent highlights its direct connections without moving the graph. Recorded Herdr
  operations and supported Codex success receipts supply directed delegation and communication links,
  with source labels, recent-activity filtering and read-only counts shared with Table. Herdr operation
  capture requires the coordination-capable Herdr fork.
- Graph view for recorded work, sessions, executions and checkouts, with search, focus and status
  filters, optional machine grouping, stable layout, details and navigation to verified live targets.
- Graph shares the app with Table and its session-move dialog. A planned move preserves the logical
  agent's selection, graph position and recorded relationships after destination verification.
